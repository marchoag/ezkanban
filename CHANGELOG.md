## 1.0.0

First release.

- **Quick capture.** Press ⌥⇧Space anywhere, even over full-screen apps, type, and hit Return. The card lands at the top of Inbox. Paste several lines to add several cards at once.
- **Menu bar board.** Inbox → Doing → Done. Drag cards between columns, or drive it from the keyboard. Press ⌥⇧K to open it from anywhere.
- **One rule.** Doing holds three cards. Finish something first.
- **Self-cleaning.** Done cards leave the board after 48 hours and are appended to `archive.jsonl`.
- **Undo.** ⌘Z undoes the last add, edit, move, or delete.
- **Plain JSON.** The board is `~/Library/Application Support/EZKanban/board.json`. Edit it by hand and the board updates live.
